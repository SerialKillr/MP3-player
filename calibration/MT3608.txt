Step 1 — Wire only the power chain first
  18650 → TP4056 → MT3608
  Do NOT connect ESP32 yet

Step 2 — Touch multimeter probes to MT3608 output
  Red probe  → MT3608 OUT+
  Black probe → MT3608 OUT−
  Turn the battery on / plug in USB to power the TP4056

Step 3 — Read the voltage
  You'll see something like 3.7V or whatever the battery is at

Step 4 — Adjust the trimpot
  The blue box on the MT3608 has a tiny cross-head screw
  Use a small flathead or Phillips screwdriver
  Turn CLOCKWISE slowly to increase voltage
  Turn ANTICLOCKWISE to decrease
  Watch the multimeter as you turn

Step 5 — Stop exactly at 5.00V
  Anything between 4.9V and 5.1V is fine
  Do not go above 5.2V — you risk damaging the ESP32

Step 6 — Done, never touch it again
  Connect ESP32 VIN to MT3608 OUT+
  Connect ESP32 GND to MT3608 OUT−
